# ʻĀina Foundry — Spring 2027 Internship flyer

Āina Foundry is the applied innovation and prototyping department of Purple Maiʻa Foundation. We build culturally grounded technology that serves ʻāina restoration and our communities—moving ideas into working prototypes and, when ready, into ventures.


‘Āina Foundry exists to turn community-vetted ideas into tangible tools and skilled people. Our purpose is to ensure Indigenous Innovation does not stop at inspiration—but moves into practice, learning, and real-world impact. We believe technology must honor place, respect ʻike, and return value to community. By pairing rapid prototyping with cultural grounding, ‘Āina Foundry supports solutions that are locally rooted, environmentally mindful, and built to last.
